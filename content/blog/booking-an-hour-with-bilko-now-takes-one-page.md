Need an hour to talk through an AI idea? You used to email Bilko, then wait, then go back and forth on a time. Now one page does the whole thing. Pick an open slot, confirm, and get a calendar invite before you close the tab.

## One page, not an email thread

Human For Hire is how founders and small teams book time with Bilko — a free first hour, then paid hours for deeper work. Until this month, the page lived at a few addresses: /bilko, /hire, /human-for-hire. Finding the right one took a search. Now all of them redirect straight to one page, bilko.run/book, and that page does the whole job.

A stepper at the top shows three steps: Time, Details, Done. Pick an open slot from the grid. Already-booked times show up greyed out and marked "Taken," so you never pick a slot that's gone. Choosing a time moves you straight into confirming your details. There's no separate scheduling tool, and no email needed to start it.

![The /book page's Pick a time step, showing open slots and one taken slot in a weekly grid](/blog-images/booking-an-hour-with-bilko-now-takes-one-page/book-time-picker.jpg "Pick a time step on bilko.run/book: open slots, one already taken, times shown in your own time zone")

## Tell Bilko what you're working on, change plans freely

On the Details step, you add your email and tap a chip for what you're bringing: New idea, Existing repo, Something's broken, or Not sure yet. That one tap tells Bilko what to expect before the call starts.

Submit, and you land on Done with a calendar invite ready for Google or Outlook. Changed your mind? The Done step lets you reschedule to a different slot or cancel, both without writing an email. Rescheduling used to confuse calendar apps — the old time could sit next to the new one instead of replacing it. That's fixed now. Every reschedule bumps the invite's version number, so your calendar app swaps in the new time instead of keeping both.

![The Details step on bilko.run/book, with an email field and four topic chips below it](/blog-images/booking-an-hour-with-bilko-now-takes-one-page/book-details-step.jpg "Details step: email field plus topic chips for New idea, Existing repo, Something's broken, Not sure yet")

## Free hour first, a clear receipt after

The first hour is still free, no card needed. What's new is what happens if you want more. Consultation, Architecture, or Design run $100 an hour after that, with a short written plan and an honest hour estimate sent first.

Pay through Stripe Checkout and you land back on /book. The page now tells you plainly what happened: "Payment received. Thank you. Bilko will email you shortly to plan your hours," if it went through, or "Checkout cancelled. No charge," if you back out. No wondering whether the charge worked.

Booking used to mean an email thread before you even had a time. Now it's one page: pick a slot, say what you're building, and leave with a plan. Paid orders already show up next to free bookings in Bilko's own admin view, so the next step is putting that data to use. Try it at [bilko.run/book](https://bilko.run/book) — the first hour costs nothing.
