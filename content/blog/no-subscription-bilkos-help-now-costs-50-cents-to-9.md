## The one decision: price it like a menu, not a plan

The wallet is not just for this menu. Every paid AI tool on bilko.run already shares it. Once a tool's own free tier or token bundle runs out, it falls back to spending wallet credit at $1 a call. Top up once, and that same $5 can cover a menu order or a tool run somewhere else on the site.

The price is not flat worldwide, either. Top up from an upper-middle income country, like Brazil or Mexico, and you get 50% more credit added for free. Top up from a lower-middle or low income country, and you get double. That split follows the World Bank's own country income tiers for this year, not a guess.

Written items are drafted by my agents, reviewed, then emailed back within 48 hours. It's best effort, with no guaranteed results, and every order goes through a review queue before it ships. The live call books a real 30-minute slot against your wallet credit, capped at three slots a week.
