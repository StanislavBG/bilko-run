Three Winds, bilko.run's newest game, is not the four-player mahjong you may already know. It plays the Malaysian three-player game instead. Before it lets you near a scored table, it gives you ten lessons with a coach and a free table to practice on.

## Three-player mahjong plays by its own rules

Most mahjong apps and most people who already play teach the four-player Chinese game. Three Winds is a different game: Malaysian three-player mahjong, 三人麻將. One seat is gone, and a winning hand is scored on its own chart, called the fan table. If you only know the four-player rules, you would sit down and lose before you understood why.

The project says plainly who it is for: people who have never played this version, from the first tile to the full fan table. It launched on November 15. It is built for someone new to this specific version, not for a mahjong veteran who needs no help.

![Three Winds landing page showing five mahjong tiles and an Enter the Salon button](/blog-images/three-winds-teaches-a-mahjong-most-people-have-never-played/tw-home.jpg "The Three Winds home screen, open to anyone with no sign-in.")

## Ten lessons with a coach before you sit down for real

Open the Academy and it reads "Ten lessons," "0 of 10 complete." Lesson 1, "The tiles," takes 6 minutes and starts with "Eighty-four tiles, three families" — the full set you will need to recognize on sight. A coach named Ms. Lin narrates each step; her line for lesson 1 is "Learn the faces first. The rest follows."

You do not have to finish any lessons before you play. The Salon lists a Practice Table marked "Free play" and "Always open," with a plain "Sit down" button and no sign-in wall. Three other rooms sit next to it, and those do ask you to sign in. So the actual way to start is simple: open the game, pick Practice Table, and play a hand while Ms. Lin's lessons wait for whenever you want them.

![Three Winds Academy page showing ten lessons and lesson 1, The tiles](/blog-images/three-winds-teaches-a-mahjong-most-people-have-never-played/tw-academy.jpg "Lesson 1 of 10, 'The tiles,' with progress at zero and a six-minute runtime.")

## Thirty-three tiles and five sets still to tell apart

The Collection page tracks every tile face you can recognize. Right now it reads "0 of 33 tiles learned," split into Dots, Winds and dragons, Flowers and seasons, and Faces, animals and the Fly. That number alone is a reason to come back; right now it is zero for everyone.

The Boutique shows five different looks for those same 33 tiles. Ivory comes included at your table. Pearl is numbered, "888 made," priced at 8,888 taels. Lacquer is marked "New," also 8,888. Jade runs 6,888, and Ink is 3,888. Taels are the game's own points, not cash. You can see all five sets clearly before you ever sign in or spend anything.

![Three Winds Boutique page listing five tile sets with tael prices](/blog-images/three-winds-teaches-a-mahjong-most-people-have-never-played/tw-boutique.jpg "Five named tile sets, each with its own look and tael price, visible without signing in.")
