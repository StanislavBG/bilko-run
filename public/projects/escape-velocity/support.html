Escape Velocity: Sol for iPhone and iPad

How to play

Fly out from Mercury through the Solar System, clear each planet's arena, shop at the Galaxy Café between planets and take on the bosses. The first level includes a guided tutorial.

Touch controls

  • Put a finger anywhere on the left side of the screen and drag to steer: a stick appears where you touch.
  • Tap DASH to dash and SPELL to cast your spell, both on the right side.
  • Tap the pause button to pause. Menus, cards and the map show their own on-screen buttons.

Controller support

You can also play with a game controller paired to your device (for example an Xbox, PlayStation or MFi controller). The left stick or D-pad moves, A dashes and confirms, X casts your spell, B or Menu pauses, and the shoulder buttons pick cards.

Saves and progress

The game saves automatically on your device. Your progress is included in your own device backups if you use them, so restoring a backup restores it. There is no account and no cloud save in the iOS app, and deleting the app deletes its progress on that device.

Sound

Open Settings in the game and use the Sound On/Off switch to turn music and sound effects on or off together. The game also follows your device volume.

Supported devices

iPhone with iOS 26 or later, and iPad with iPadOS 26 or later.
