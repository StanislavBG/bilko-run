How days are counted

The day you set the eggs is day 0. For chickens, lockdown is day 18 and hatch day is day 21. All dates come from your set date, so they never drift.

I am not getting reminders

Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Hatch Day and allow notifications. Reminders arrive at 9 AM local time (the lockdown-eve reminder at 6 PM). Turning reminders are skipped when "Automatic egg turner" is on.

Restoring the unlock

On a new phone, open Hatch Day → Unlock unlimited hatches → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

My data

Everything is stored only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud copy.
